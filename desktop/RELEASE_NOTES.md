IdleCardShop for Windows and Linux, built with Godot 4.7.2.

- The whole browser game: every tab, the tutorial, market, staff, storage, properties, marketing, finances, achievements and 11 themes.
- Pixel art shop floor, booster opening, calm music and sound.
- Offline time is simulated in full on a background thread.
- Import your browser save in Settings. Saves move both ways.

Unzip and run `IdleCardShop.exe` (Windows) or `IdleCardShop.x86_64` (Linux). Keep the `packs` folder next to the game: it holds the card images.
